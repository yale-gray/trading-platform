# Audit 008: Wave 7 (ADR-0001 section 8, A23-A29)

Verdict: **FAIL (1 blocker, 8 majors, 9 minors)**. Fix the blocker before the Tier A batch is run; otherwise gate 7 cannot pass for any strategy. `uv run pytest -q -m "not slow"`: 2444 passed; ruff clean. Experiments were run from scratch scripts (scratchpad), no source edited.

## Blocker

**B1. Gate 7 (DSR) is unattainable with the seeded registry.** `validation.py` run_validation (the `ts = [...]` block, ~line 1181) feeds `deflated_sharpe` the variance of ALL registry Sharpes. The 4 seeded trials are annual Sharpes -1.5..-4.1 divided by sqrt(252), a stated approximation from a different (15m, 5 bp) regime. With them plus a +1.0 annual candidate, V = 0.0143 and N = 12 gives SR0 = 0.199 per day = **3.16 annualised Sharpe** (about 3.0 with five more plausible batch Sharpes). Nothing can reach DSR >= 0.95. With the null-variance V ~ 1/T the same N gives SR0 ~ 0.59 annualised. The formula itself (`protocol.py:207-268`: sqrt(V)*((1-g)Z^-1(1-1/N)+g Z^-1(1-1/(Ne)), denominator 1 - skew*SR + (kurt-1)/4*SR^2 with non-excess kurtosis, sqrt(T-1)) is correct; the input is the problem.
Fix: take V from same-protocol trials only (exclude `status="failed"` seed rows from V but keep them in N), or use V = max(sample V, 1/(T-1)) capped, or the batch's own Sharpes. Decide and record it in the ADR before the run. Also make the result order-independent: V currently depends on which batch members have already had `sharpe_period` appended.

## Majors

- **M1. A4 as briefed has no exit, so it round-trips in one bar.** `engine.py:540-547`: with no `exit`, a held symbol exits when "entry no longer true", and entry is true only on bar 0. Reproduced: BUY 10:15 ET, SELL 10:30 ET (a 15-minute trade). Add an explicit never-true exit, for example `{gt: [{session: bar_index}, 99]}`; the flatten account then closes at the session end (reproduced: BUY 15:15 UTC, SELL 20:45 UTC). Also `weight: "equal"` is required with `max_positions` (weight 1.0 x 5 is rejected).
- **M2. T+1 cash settlement silently halves A2 and starves A1.** Reproduced A2 on the swing account: with `settlement="none"` it trades every night (buy 15:45, sell 09:30); with `"t1"` it trades every other night (the 09:30 sale's proceeds are unsettled for the 15:45 buy; no reject is logged). A1 under t1: 50 vs 60 trades in a 2-month toy and idle cash. Use `settlement="none"` for A1, A2, A6 (and state it in the pre-registration); a day-trade-limited cash account makes A2 a different strategy.
- **M3. A5 and A6 cannot pass gate 4 (min_trades=100).** A5 yields ~93 round trips over 2016-03..2023-12 (measured 93); A6 is 8 FOMC/yr, about 62. They can never get "advance". Pre-register a different trade floor for event/calendar strategies (and say so), or accept they are reported but not eligible.
- **M4. `cost_profile` is not in `config_hash`** (`backtest/engine.py:_config_payload`, 156-198). flat, tiered and tiered_stress runs hash identically, so the hold-out log (`open_holdout`, `config_hash(cfg)`), result caches and checkpoints cannot tell them apart. Add `cost_profile` to `flags` when != "flat" (keeps goldens).
- **M5. Noise band has NaN pockets after every half day** (`indicators.py:_noise_sigma`, `rolling(n, min_periods=n)`). A half-day (14 bars) puts a NaN in bars 14-25 for the next 14 sessions: measured 12 of 26 bars NaN for 14 sessions after 2023-11-24, about 40 sessions/yr (17%). In those windows A3's entry never fires and the `close < noise_upper` exit is false (only vwap exits). Not lookahead, but a silent, biased sample. Fix: skip half-day sessions in sigma, or use nanmean with min_periods ~ n-3.
- **M6. A1 and A4 universe is survivorship-biased.** `data/universes/sp500.meta.json`: current constituents only (snapshot 2026-10-21). Both are buy-the-loser reversal strategies, where the bias is upward and large (losers that were later delisted/removed are missing). Any pass is an upper bound; label it in the report, and do not read DSR on A1/A4 as clean. Prefer tiered_stress for these (gap-down opens have far wider spreads than the 1.5-3 bp flat).
- **M7. Gate 5 is vacuous for parameter-less strategies and destructive for bar-index parameters.** A strategy with no `$params` gets only two "corner" variants identical to base (measured: 2 variants, 0 skipped), so gate 5 passes trivially. If bar indices are made parameters, +-20% of 24 gives 19/29 (29 does not exist, so zero trades, collapse); 0 is "below 1". Keep bar indices and FOMC offsets as literals, and treat gate 5 as "not informative" for A2/A5/A6 in the write-up.
- **M8. SPY overlay silently becomes None for A1/A4** unless SPY daily/15m bars are passed. `run_validation` uses `spy_bars` or `canon.get("SPY")`; the S&P 500 universe does not contain SPY, and `protocol_verdict` then reports "overlay adds no value" with no hint. Pass `spy_bars` (daily for A1, 15m for A4), already truncated or not (it is truncated inside).

## Minors

- m1. **A6 exit fills at the announcement.** Exit decided on bar 17 (13:45-14:00) fills at bar 18's open, i.e. the first print at/after 14:00:00, when the statement is released; the price may already be post-news. Entry (decided bar 17, fill 14:00 the day before) is fine. To exit pre-announcement decide on bar 16 (fill 13:45).
- m2. A1 `bars_held >= 5` holds 6 sessions open-to-open (decision on the 5th close after the fill day, fills the 6th open); use `>= 4` for a 5-day hold. Each cohort is rebought one day after the sells (sell proceeds only fund buys decided next bar): about 1 idle day per cycle. Partial trims to target weight add small sells/buys and inflate trade counts and costs (pre-existing engine behaviour).
- m3. `bar_index` is a per-session row count, not time-of-day. A missing 15m bar shifts every later index; A2's exit (25) then never occurs and the position stays an extra night. Add a "26 bars per full session / 14 per half day" data check for SPY/QQQ. Half days (no bar 24/25, no bar 17 on a 14-bar day) simply skip, which is fine.
- m4. Crypto under "tiered": fee 10 bp (CostConfig) + 15 bp slip = 25 bp per side, vs the ADR intent of about 15 bp total (the flat default was 20). Recommend `tier_extra_fee_bps` return a negative offset, or set the crypto fee to 5 bp under tiered profiles. Not used by Tier A.
- m5. Risk sizing/`reserved_cash` still uses the flat per-class cost (5 bp equity). Acceptable: tiered is mostly cheaper (reserve is conservative) and `_fill` re-checks affordability with the real fee and slip, so over-spend is impossible.
- m6. Kill switch re-arm is `pause_bars=20` bars. On a 15m swing account that is 5 hours, not days, and it can re-arm the next morning. Pre-existing; consider scaling to sessions for swing.
- m7. FOMC list: all 2015-2027 scheduled statement dates verified against the Fed calendar; 2020-03-18 is absent on purpose (the meeting was replaced by the 03-15 unscheduled one). Fine, but note the omission in the data-source line.
- m8. `open_holdout` does not check the registry status or the protocol verdict, and `batch` is a free string (a differently spelled batch resets the 2-finalist limit). Take the batch name from the registry record and require `status == "advanced"`.
- m9. The 50-name mega-cap list is a 2026 snapshot applied to 2016-2023 (INTC, T, VZ, PFE were mega then, others were not); effect is small (1.5 vs 3 bp).

## Checks that passed

1. **Lookahead.** I recomputed prev_close, gap, vwap, tday_of_month, tdays_to_month_end, fomc tdays_until, noise_upper/lower and bar_index on a full 15m frame and on prefixes truncated mid-session and mid-month: **0 mismatching rows in all 27 comparisons**. Calendar and FOMC values come from `xnys_sessions` and `fomc.csv`, not from data presence (a month cut mid-way still sees the full month). Noise sigma reads only earlier sessions (`shift(1)`). Lag 0 fills at t+1's open, never t: reproduced A2 (decision bar 24, BUY at 15:45 ET bar 25 open; decision bar 25, SELL at next 09:30 open); the broker only fills orders with `created_asof < asof`. `clock_only()` inspects the full body dump (rank, filters, stops), so any price/indicator/pos/other-session operand returns False; a clock-only rule cannot use post-close information.
2. **Swing.** `_flatten`-gated paths (`late_flatten`, session-end clear, cross-session decision drop) are inert when flatten is off; engine session_end/late_flatten also require `intraday_flatten`. Pending orders carry across the session (reproduced) and `max_pending_bars` counts only bars where the symbol has no bar. Kill switch: see m6.
3. **Protocol math.** Holm (`holm_adjust`): (m-rank)*p with running max, so monotone; NaN treated as 1. Newey-West: Bartlett weights 1-l/(L+1), 5 lags, HAC covariance has the correct n scaling. T-bill: DTB3 percent to decimal, forward-filled, daily = annual/252 (365 for crypto), one-sided p = 1 - Phi(t). Overlay: inner join on date, OLS with HAC, 50/50 daily mix. Hold-out: `canon` and `spy_bars` are cut at `< 2024-01-01 UTC` and `cfg.end` clamped before the calendar, split, history check, walk-forward, sensitivity, OOS and fund run; verified on a 2015-2025 synthetic run (split ended 2023-12-29). `open_holdout` logs before running, refuses repeats, caps 2 per batch.
4. **Costs.** Tier figures in `cost_tiers.py` match A23 (1/3/1.5/3/15/30 bp, 0.25 bp sell-only equity fee, stress x2 on slippage only). BRK.B/BRK-B normalise to the listed symbol. "flat" is omitted from serialisation, goldens unchanged.

## Item 5: will the six Tier A strategies work as briefed?

- **A1 (daily reversal):** `always` + rank + `max_positions` works (10 names, cohort rotation). Needs `settlement: none` (M2), explicit SPY bars (M8), survivorship caveat (M6); `bars_held >= 4` for a true 5-day hold (m2). Daily cohort trims inflate trades slightly.
- **A2 (overnight 15m swing):** entry 24 then exit 25 on consecutive bars works exactly as intended with lag 0 and `settlement: none`, `intraday_flatten: false`. With t1 it trades every other night (M2). No parameters so gate 5 is vacuous (M7); keep literals. Early-close days are skipped.
- **A3 (noise-band momentum, flatten):** works, normal lag 2, flatten at close. NaN pockets after half days (M5) cut about 17% of late-session signals; re-entry after an exit on the same day is possible. Needs tiered_stress as the headline cost case.
- **A4 (gap-down reversal):** broken as briefed (M1): add the never-true exit and `weight: "equal"`. Survivorship bias (M6). Lag 2 means fills at bar 3 open (10:15 ET).
- **A5 (turn of month, daily):** operands correct and causal; buys the last session's open, sells the 4th session's open. At most ~93 round trips so it cannot pass gate 4 (M3); gate 5 vacuous (M7).
- **A6 (pre-FOMC, 15m swing):** operands correct; ~62 trips, cannot pass gate 4 (M3); exit fills at the 14:00 announcement print (m1); needs `settlement: none`.
